# References

These are the six papers listed on slide 12 of the original presentation. DOI, title, year and journal metadata were checked against publisher or institutional records on October 20, 2026. Links provide access to the sources; this repository does not redistribute the papers.

1. Lichtman, J. W., & Conchello, J.-A. (2005). Fluorescence microscopy. *Nature Methods, 2*, 910–919. https://doi.org/10.1038/nmeth817

2. Torrado, B., Pannunzio, B., Malacrida, L., & Digman, M. A. (2024). Fluorescence lifetime imaging microscopy. *Nature Reviews Methods Primers, 4*, Article 80. https://doi.org/10.1038/s43586-024-00358-8

3. Ha, T., Fei, J., Schmid, S., Lee, N. K., Gonzalez, R. L., Jr., Paul, S., & Yeou, S. (2024). Fluorescence resonance energy transfer at the single-molecule level. *Nature Reviews Methods Primers, 4*, Article 21. https://doi.org/10.1038/s43586-024-00298-3

4. Agam, G., et al. (2023). Reliability and accuracy of single-molecule FRET studies for characterization of structural dynamics and distances in proteins. *Nature Methods, 20*, 523–535. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-023-01807-0

5. Lelek, M., Gyparaki, M. T., Beliu, G., Schueder, F., Griffié, J., Manley, S., Jungmann, R., Sauer, M., Lakadamyali, M., & Zimmer, C. (2021). Single-molecule localization microscopy. *Nature Reviews Methods Primers, 1*, Article 39. https://doi.org/10.1038/s43586-021-00038-x

6. Schermelleh, L., Ferrand, A., Huser, T., Eggeling, C., Sauer, M., Biehlmaier, O., & Drummen, G. P. C. (2019). Super-resolution microscopy demystified. *Nature Cell Biology, 21*, 72–84. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41556-018-0251-8

## Published correction

Lelek et al. published an **Author Correction** (2022), *Nature Reviews Methods Primers, 2*, Article 70: https://doi.org/10.1038/s43586-022-00161-3. It corrects a localization-precision equation. That equation is not reproduced in this project's README or supplied slides; consult the corrected source for quantitative use.
